Studify CA - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026. Studify CA is a free study planner for CA students. It is not an ICAI product.

What stays on your device

Your study plan, chapters, timer sessions, notes and marks are saved in your browser or app on your device.

If you sign in

A copy of your progress is saved in your cloud account (Supabase) so it syncs across your devices. We store your email, and the ICAI registration number and name you enter. These are seen only by app admins to verify you. One ICAI number works for one account. We never ask for your ICAI password.

What other people can see

Partners and group members see only the items you switch on. If you join the leaderboard, everyone sees only your nickname, study time and streak. You can leave the leaderboard and switch off sharing at any time.

AI features

When you use AI tools (tutor, homework check, video notes), the text, image or video link you submit is sent to Google Gemini to produce the answer. Do not submit anything you want to keep private.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, we store the technical token needed to send you reminders and partner nudges. You can turn them off in the app or your phone settings.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data and we show no ads.

Delete your data

Open Settings, then Account, then Delete account. This removes your account and the data saved in the cloud. You can also email us to ask for deletion.

Accuracy

Dates, chapter lists and papers can be wrong. Confirm on icai.org.

Contact
